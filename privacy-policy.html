MAN MODE / STORE INFORMATION

PRIVACY POLICY.

This policy explains how the store may collect and use information when customers browse, place an order or contact the business.

Information collected

Order information may include customer name, phone number, delivery address, district and purchased items. The store should collect only information needed to fulfil orders and provide support.

How information is used

  • To confirm and deliver customer orders.
  • To contact customers about an order or support request.
  • To maintain store records and prevent misuse.

Sharing and security

Do not sell customer personal information. Share it only with service providers necessary to operate the store, such as delivery partners, and protect access to order records.

Retention and customer requests

Set a retention period that meets local law and business needs. Provide a working contact method for customers to request access or correction of their information.

Store owner: customize this draft before publishing the store for real customers. Add your actual business identity, contact details, delivery coverage, return window and applicable legal requirements. This starter text is not legal advice.
