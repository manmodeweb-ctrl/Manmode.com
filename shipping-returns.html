MAN MODE / STORE INFORMATION

SHIPPING & RETURNS.

The store owner should specify the areas served, delivery partners, delivery time estimates and charges before accepting live orders.

Delivery charges and timing

Delivery fees may vary by location. Any free-delivery threshold and delivery estimates should be shown before the customer places an order. Contact the customer if delivery cannot be completed as expected.

Order confirmation

Orders are subject to stock availability and confirmation by the store. Keep customers informed if an order is delayed, changed or cancelled.

Returns, exchanges and refunds

Set the return and exchange window, eligible product conditions, process for damaged or incorrect items, and refund method. Clearly state any exclusions only where permitted by law.

Damaged or incorrect orders

Give customers a clear way to report a damaged, missing or incorrect item, including the information or photographs they may need to provide.

Store owner: customize this draft before publishing the store for real customers. Add your actual business identity, contact details, delivery coverage, return window and applicable legal requirements. This starter text is not legal advice.
